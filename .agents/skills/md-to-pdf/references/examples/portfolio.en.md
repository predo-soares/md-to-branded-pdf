Two synthetic case studies illustrate the portfolio structure. They do not describe real clients or outcomes.

## Case Study A: Onboarding Flow

### Context

A fictional product team wanted to understand where new users hesitated during setup. The source brief defined a small study and a limited review window.

![Synthetic onboarding research stages](figure.svg)
*Figure 1. A sample research sequence used only for layout testing.*

### Approach

The sample team mapped the first-run path, observed task attempts, and grouped notes by the point where help was needed. A [research methods reference](https://example.org/methods) was available for comparison.

### Outcome

The synthetic exercise produced a list of questions for a later experiment. No product improvement metric is claimed.
